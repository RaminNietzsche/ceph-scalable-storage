About this work. This book is an independent study guide and is not a translation of the original book. Only the original's public table of contents was used to order the topics. The text, explanations, examples, commands, exercises and all figures were written and designed for this guide. No text or image from the original book is reproduced here. The English edition is a translation of the author's original Persian text.

Original book. Vikhyat Umrao, Karan Singh, Nick Fisk, Anthony D'Atri, Vaibhav Bhembre, Ceph: Designing and Implementing Scalable Storage Systems, 2019 (ISBN 9781788295413).
